# Publications & Scientific Literature

This directory houses all research papers, manuscripts, and publications related to this project, organized by domain.

## Organization
- `system/`: Neurosymbolic Pipeline paper (system identification, SINDY, PINN-Sigma, etc.)
- `thermal/`: Orbital Thermal Simulator and Emulator paper (PINN-based emulations)
- `qg/`: Toy models for Quantum Gravity and spin network geometric audits
