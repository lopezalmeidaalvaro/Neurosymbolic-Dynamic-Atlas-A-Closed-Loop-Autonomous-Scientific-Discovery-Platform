## 1. Introduction and Objectives
To establish a truly unified description of nature, we must determine if there exists an irreducible informational entity—a "minimal informational atom" $I_0$—that is more fundamental than spacetime geometry, quantum states, entropy, complexity, and causality, and from which all of them can be derived as emergent limits:

This document audits the primary candidates for this fundamental atom: Wheeler's classical binary bit, the quantum qubit, nodes of quantum graphity, spin-network nodes/punctures, causal set elements, process matrix events, cells in quantum cellular automata, and tensor network blocks. ## 2. Audit of Irreducible Informational Candidates

We evaluate the primary candidates for the irreducible informational atom:

### 2.1 Wheeler's "It-from-Bit" (Classical Bit)
- **Concept**: Spacetime and matter emerge from binary classical decisions.
- **Evaluation**: Too simple. A classical bit cannot represent quantum superposition or entanglement, which are essential to reconstruct the holographic connections of spacetime.

### 2.2 The Qubit (Quantum Bit / Qubit Ontology)
- **Concept**: The fundamental building block is a two-state quantum system $|\psi\rangle = \alpha |0\rangle + \beta |1\rangle$.
- **Evaluation**: Incorporates superposition and entanglement. However, a set of qubits requires a pre-existing Hilbert space structure to define their tensor product, which violates the requirement that the Hilbert space itself must emerge.

### 2.3 Spin-Network Nodes and Punctures
- **Concept**: Edge punctures on boundaries carry spin representations $j_p$ and magnetic numbers $m_p$.
- **Evaluation**: Excellent for area and volume discretization. However, they are defined within the group-theoretic structure of $SU(2)$, which is a specific mathematical choice rather than a pure informational property.

### 2.4 Causal Set Elements (Events)
- **Concept**: Irreducible mathematical events partially ordered by causal relations.
- **Evaluation**: Successfully reconstructs volume (number of events) and causal conformal structure. However, it lacks a natural way to represent quantum superposition and state interference.

### 2.5 Process Matrix Events (Indefinite Causal Events)
- **Concept**: Local quantum operations without a fixed global causal order.
- **Evaluation**: Incorporates quantum mechanics and causal indefiniteness, but is mathematically formulated as an operator mapping rather than a discrete building block.

### 2.6 Quantum Cellular Automata (QCA) Cells
- **Concept**: A discrete lattice of cells where each cell updates its state based on a local quantum transition rule.
- **Evaluation**: Excellent for modeling emergent dynamics and field equations, but assumes a fixed background lattice.

## 3. Proposal for the Minimal Informational Atom ($I_0$)

We define the fundamental informational atom $I_0$ as a **Relational Quantum Bit-Event (RQB-Event)**:

1.  **Definition**: An $I_0$ is an abstract event carrying:
    - A binary quantum state $|s\rangle \in \mathbb{C}^2$ (a qubit of information).
    - An adjacency relation (entanglement bond) linking it to other events.
2.  **State Representation**:
    $$|I_0\rangle = \alpha |0\rangle_i + \beta |1\rangle_i$$
3.  **Emergence Mapping**:
    - **Entanglement ($E$)**: Emerges from the bipartite entanglement between different $I_0$ events.
    - **Spacetime Geometry ($g$)**: Reconstructed from the network of these entanglement bonds (tensor network representation).
    - **Causality**: Reconstructed from the directed directed acyclic graph of transitions between RQB-Events.
    - **Quantum State ($\Psi$)**: The collective state of the many-node $I_0$ network.

In the Hayward-LQC stable remnant, the finite geometry is composed of a finite assembly of these RQB-Events ($N_{\text{micro}} \approx 1174$), which prevents the volume from collapsing below the Planck scale.
