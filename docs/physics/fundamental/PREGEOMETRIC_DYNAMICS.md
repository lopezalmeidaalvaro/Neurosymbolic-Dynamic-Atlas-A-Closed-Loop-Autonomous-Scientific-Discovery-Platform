# Pregeometric Dynamics for Hayward-LQC

## 1. Introduction and Objectives
To find a dynamics that exists prior to geometry, metric structures, and classical coordinate time, we must describe how the fundamental informational atom $I_0$ (represented as a network of Relational Quantum Bit-Events, RQB-Events) evolves. We seek a pregeometric dynamics equation of the form:
$$\frac{d\mathcal{I}}{d\tau} = \mathcal{F}(\mathcal{I})$$
where $\mathcal{I}$ is the state operator of the informational network, $\tau$ is a dimensionless relational ordering parameter, and $\mathcal{F}$ is a coordinate-free quantum transition mapping. 

This document audits candidate pregeometric dynamics theories—Spin Network Evolution, Group Field Theory (GFT), Quantum Graph Rewriting, Causal Dynamical Triangulations (CDT), Tensor Network Evolution, and Quantum Cellular Automata (QCA)—and formulates a concrete mathematical proposal.

## 2. Audit of Pregeometric Dynamics Models

We evaluate the candidate frameworks based on their background independence, lack of classical spacetime coordinates, and capacity to reproduce general relativity in the infrared limit:

### 2.1 Spin Network Evolution (Spinfoam transitions)
- **Concept**: States are spatial spin networks; dynamics are defined by spinfoam transition amplitudes summing over histories of spin networks.
- **Evaluation**: Fully background-independent. However, it relies heavily on the group representation theory of $SU(2)$ or $SL(2,\mathbb{C})$, which is a specific mathematical structure rather than pure relational information.

### 2.2 Group Field Theory (GFT)
- **Concept**: A field theory defined on a group manifold where fields create/annihilate quantum tetrahedra.
- **Evaluation**: The partition function generates spinfoams. It is background-independent and allows for thermodynamic treatment of geometry, but still assumes a background group manifold.

### 2.3 Quantum Graph Rewriting (Quantum Graphity)
- **Concept**: Graph models where the vertices are quantum states and edges can be created, destroyed, or rewritten according to a local Hamiltonian.
- **Evaluation**: Spacetime geometry emerges as a low-temperature phase where the graph becomes ordered and low-dimensional. Excellent for modeling the transition from a pregeometric state to a spatial geometry.

### 2.4 Causal Dynamical Triangulations (CDT)
- **Concept**: Path integral over simplicial complexes with a strict causal structure, maintaining a global time foliation.
- **Evaluation**: Highly successful in producing a classical de Sitter spacetime limit. However, the introduction of a global time foliation and the reliance on triangles (simplicial manifolds) introduces geometric assumptions prior to the dynamics.

### 2.5 Tensor Network Evolution
- **Concept**: Dynamic entanglement renormalization (e.g., MERA or tensor networks under unitary gates) where the depth of the network corresponds to the flow of time.
- **Evaluation**: Elegant connection to the holographic principle. However, most tensor network models require a fixed boundary geometry or background lattice to define the layout.

### 2.6 Quantum Cellular Automata (QCA)
- **Concept**: Unitary updates on a lattice of cells containing qubits.
- **Evaluation**: Excellent for local causal propagation, but typically formulated on flat background lattices, making background independence difficult to achieve.

## 3. Proposal for the Pregeometric Dynamics Equation

Let the collective informational state of the network of $N$ RQB-Events be represented by the relational density matrix $\rho(\tau)$ acting on the Hilbert space $\mathcal{H}_i^{\otimes N} \simeq (\mathbb{C}^2)^{\otimes N}$ of the qubits, where the connectivity of the network is represented by a relational adjacency operator $\hat{A}$.

We define the **Pregeometric Dynamics Equation** as:
$$\frac{d\rho(\tau)}{d\tau} = \mathcal{L}_{\text{pre}}[\rho(\tau)]$$

where:
1.  **Relational Parameter ($\tau$)**: A dimensionless ordering parameter corresponding to the flow generated by the Tomita-Takesaki modular Hamiltonian of the state, or the discrete count of graph updates.
2.  **Pregeometric Liouvillian ($\mathcal{L}_{\text{pre}}$)**: A Lindbladian operator that generates both unitary transitions and dissipation (decoherence/entanglement generation):
    $$\mathcal{L}_{\text{pre}}[\rho] = -i [\hat{H}_{\text{rel}}, \rho] + \sum_k \left( \hat{L}_k \rho \hat{L}_k^\dagger - \frac{1}{2} \{\hat{L}_k^\dagger \hat{L}_k, \rho\} \right)$$
    -   **Relational Hamiltonian ($\hat{H}_{\text{rel}}$)**: Governs the local unitary rotation of the qubit states, coupled to the relational adjacency operator $\hat{A}$:
        $$\hat{H}_{\text{rel}} = \sum_{i,j} \hat{A}_{ij} \vec{\sigma}_i \cdot \vec{\sigma}_j$$
        where $\vec{\sigma}_i$ are the Pauli matrices of the $i$-th RQB-Event.
    -   **Jump Operators ($\hat{L}_k$)**: Govern the creation and destruction of entanglement bonds between events, driven by the alignment of their states:
        $$\hat{L}_{ij} = \gamma_{\text{bond}} \left( |11\rangle\langle 00|_{ij} \hat{A}_{ij} + |00\rangle\langle 11|_{ij} (\mathbb{I} - \hat{A}_{ij}) \right)$$

For the Hayward-LQC black hole remnant, this equation describes how a finite configuration of $N_{\text{micro}} \approx 1174$ nodes evolves at the core. At the bounce point, the density matrix $\rho(\tau)$ reaches a maximum entanglement entropy state, halting the geometric collapse due to the LQC area gap and initiating the transition to the emergent white hole phase.
