# Neutrino Squared Mass Differences from RQB

## 1. Introduction and Objectives
The objective of this document is to calculate the neutrino squared mass differences $\Delta m_{21}^2$ and $\Delta m_{31}^2$ from the derived neutrino masses, comparing them to experimental data.

$$\Delta m_{21}^2 = m_2^2 - m_1^2 \quad \text{and} \quad \Delta m_{31}^2 = m_3^2 - m_1^2$$

No experimental values are used during the derivation.

---

## 2. Calculation of Squared Mass Differences

Using the derived masses:
-   $m_1 \approx 0.00308 \text{ eV}$
-   $m_2 \approx 0.01250 \text{ eV}$
-   $m_3 \approx 0.05023 \text{ eV}$

We compute the squared mass differences:

### 2.1 Solar Mass Difference ($\Delta m_{21}^2$)
$$\Delta m_{21}^2 = m_2^2 - m_1^2 = (0.01250 \text{ eV})^2 - (0.00308 \text{ eV})^2 = 1.5625 \times 10^{-4} - 9.486 \times 10^{-6} \approx 1.468 \times 10^{-4} \text{ eV}^2$$

Therefore:
$$\Delta m_{21}^2 \approx 1.47 \times 10^{-4} \text{ eV}^2$$

### 2.2 Atmospheric Mass Difference ($\Delta m_{31}^2$)
$$\Delta m_{31}^2 = m_3^2 - m_1^2 = (0.05023 \text{ eV})^2 - (0.00308 \text{ eV})^2 = 2.523 \times 10^{-3} - 9.486 \times 10^{-6} \approx 2.514 \times 10^{-3} \text{ eV}^2$$

Therefore:
$$\Delta m_{31}^2 \approx 2.51 \times 10^{-3} \text{ eV}^2$$
