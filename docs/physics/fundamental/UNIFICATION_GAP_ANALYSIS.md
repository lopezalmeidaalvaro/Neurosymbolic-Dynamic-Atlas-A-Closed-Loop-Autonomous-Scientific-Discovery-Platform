## 3. Detailed Gap Analysis

### 3.1 Matter-Gravity Coupling
In the effective Hayward-LQC metric, matter is coupled classically via the stress-energy tensor. However, in the fundamental quantum theory, matter fields (fermions, gauge bosons) must be represented as excitations of the spin-network graph (e.g., nodes carrying matter representations). The current GFT and spin foam models lack a complete, anomaly-free vertex amplitude that incorporates both gravitational and Standard Model matter fields.

### 3.2 The Cosmological Constant ($\Lambda$)
The cosmological constant is essential to explain the accelerated expansion of the universe. In GFT and spin foams, $\Lambda$ is introduced by using quantum groups (like $U_q(SL(2,\mathbb{C}))$) at the vertex, where $q = e^{i\Lambda l_P^2}$. While this provides a mathematical regularization, a physical derivation of why $\Lambda$ is so small (the cosmological constant problem) remains entirely unresolved.
