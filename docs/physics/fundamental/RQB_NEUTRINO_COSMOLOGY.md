# Cosmological Consistency of the RQB Neutrino Sector

## 1. Introduction and Objectives
The objective of this document is to evaluate the cosmological compatibility of the RQB-derived neutrino mass spectrum ($m_1, m_2, m_3$) and the heavy sterile scale $M_{R, n}$ with constraints from the Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB), structure formation, and big bang nucleosynthesis (BBN).

---

## 2. Active Neutrino Constraints

### 2.1 The Mass Sum Constraint ($\sum m_\nu$)
Cosmological measurements from the Planck satellite (2018 data) constrain the sum of active neutrino masses:
$$\sum m_\nu < 0.12 \text{ eV} \quad (95\% \text{ CL, Planck TT,TE,EE+lowE+lensing+BAO})$$

In the RQB normal hierarchy spectrum:
-   $m_1 \approx 0.0031 \text{ eV}$
-   $m_2 \approx 0.0124 \text{ eV}$
-   $m_3 \approx 0.0501 \text{ eV}$
-   $\sum m_\nu = m_1 + m_2 + m_3 \approx 0.0658 \text{ eV}$

Our derived mass sum is $\sum m_\nu \approx 0.0658 \text{ eV}$, which is well below the Planck upper limit and sits just above the absolute minimum allowed by the normal hierarchy ($\approx 0.06 \text{ eV}$). This is fully consistent with CMB observations.

### 2.2 Neutrino Free-Streaming and Structure Formation
Light neutrinos free-stream in the early universe, suppressing the matter power spectrum $P(k)$ at small scales. The RQB active neutrino masses are light enough to ensure that the suppression is minimal, in agreement with Large Scale Structure (LSS) surveys such as DESI and EUCLID.

## 3. Sterile/Right-Handed Neutrino Constraints

### 3.1 Relic Abundance and BBN
Right-handed sterile neutrinos can act as hot/warm dark matter or alter the effective number of neutrino species ($N_{\text{eff}}$) during BBN if they are light ($\sim \text{eV}$ to $\text{keV}$) and stable.

However, in the RQB model, the sterile states are heavy:
-   $M_{R, 1} \approx 75.6 \text{ GeV}$
-   $M_{R, 2} \approx 304.8 \text{ GeV}$
-   $M_{R, 3} \approx 1.23 \text{ TeV}$

### 3.2 Decay and Thermalization
Because their masses are above the electroweak scale:
-   They decay rapidly via weak interactions ($N_1 \to L W, \nu Z, \nu H$) in the early universe.
-   Their lifetime is extremely short ($\tau \sim 10^{-12} \text{ s}$), decaying at temperatures $T \sim 100 \text{ GeV}$.
-   They decouple and decay long before BBN ($T \sim 1 \text{ MeV}$) and CMB recombination ($T \sim 0.3 \text{ eV}$).

Therefore, they leave no sterile relics in the universe, preserve the standard value $N_{\text{eff}} \approx 3.046$, and do not interfere with structure formation or light element abundances.

## 4. Conclusion
The RQB active and sterile neutrino sectors are fully compatible with all cosmological constraints, including Planck CMB mass bounds, free-streaming limits, and BBN $N_{\text{eff}}$ limits.
