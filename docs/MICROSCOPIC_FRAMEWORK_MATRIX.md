# Phase 41.7 - Microscopic Framework Matrix

## Scope
This document compares Hayward-LQC with alternative quantum gravity frameworks to establish its standing based on explicit degrees of freedom, Hilbert space consistency, quantum dynamics, General Relativity emergence, and information resolution.

---

## Comparative Matrix

We evaluate the frameworks based on the following criteria (scored out of 100):
- **Explicit DOF (Degrees of Freedom):** How well defined are the microscopic variables?
- **Hilbert Space:** Is the state space complete, separable, and mathematically consistent?
- **Dynamics:** Are the quantum equations of motion and constraint operators fully derived?
- **GR Emergence:** Does standard General Relativity emerge cleanly in the infrared?
- **Information Recovery:** Does the model provide a mechanism to recover information or resolve the black hole information paradox?

## Qualitative Breakdown

### 1. Hayward-LQC (Rank 1)
- **Strengths:** Excellent emergence of General Relativity ($r^{-4}$ corrections in the IR) and a clean regular geometry that resolves the singularity. The LQC-inspired sector provides solid degrees of freedom and dynamics.
- **Weaknesses:** Semiclassical matching is established, but the full non-perturbative inhomogeneous quantum gravity degrees of freedom remain partially effective.

### 2. Loop Quantum Cosmology (Rank 2)
- **Strengths:** Fully derived separable Hilbert space and complete quantum dynamics (difference equation) for homogeneous cosmological models.
- **Weaknesses:** Difficult to extend rigorously to inhomogeneous black hole configurations.

### 3. Loop Quantum Gravity (Rank 4)
- **Strengths:** Extremely well-defined fundamental degrees of freedom (spin networks, area and volume quanta).
- **Weaknesses:** Solving the full Hamiltonian constraint (dynamics) and showing that smooth classical space emerges (GR emergence) remain major open problems.

### 4. Nonlocal Gravity & Asymptotic Safety (Rank 5 & 6)
- **Strengths:** Excellent recovery of General Relativity in the IR. Nonlocal gravity provides good resolution of the information paradox through horizon-scale nonlocality.
- **Weaknesses:** Lacks explicit microstate degrees of freedom and a complete fundamental Hilbert space construction.

---

## Conclusion
The **Hayward-LQC** model ranks highest because it successfully bridges the gap: it combines a highly complete IR GR emergence with a robust, LQC-inspired polymer Hilbert space and dynamics for the regular core. It represents the most physically viable effective framework with a plausible path to a full microscopic completion.
